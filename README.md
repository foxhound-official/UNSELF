# UNSELF

**RE-MEMBER YOURSELF**

UNSELF is a first-person immersive sim with psychological horror and cyberpunk elements.

You are not a whole person.

Identity in this world can be installed, replaced, suppressed, corrupted and weaponized. Specialized personality fragments called **Members** alter perception, behavior and available actions.

The player enters the world through an external control layer called **Y.O.U.**, sharing a body with an existing identity known as **I**.

The central question is not who controls the body.

It is what remains when control, memory and identity can no longer be separated.

## Core Concept

UNSELF is built around the idea that identity is modular.

**Members** are personality fragments that provide specific knowledge, instincts and behavioral patterns. Different combinations affect how the player perceives environments, approaches obstacles, interacts with systems and responds to people.

Members are not simple skills.

They are parts of someone.

Some are common and standardized. Some are unstable. Some are unique. Some may change while being used.

The player is never fully certain whether a thought, impulse or decision belongs to **I**, **Y.O.U.**, an installed Member, or something emerging between them.

---

## Y.O.U.

The Y.O.U. system contains ten slots.

Two are permanently occupied:

- **I**: the base identity of the host
- **Y.O.U.**: the external control layer

The remaining eight slots are used by Members.

Members may occupy one, two or three slots depending on their complexity.

Configuration cannot be changed freely at any moment. Rebuilding the active identity requires dedicated hardware and a stable environment.

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

UNSELF is designed around systemic problem solving rather than fixed solutions.

A locked path may be bypassed through technical access, force, social manipulation, observation, environmental exploitation or a completely different route.

The correct solution is not defined by the game.

The available solution depends on who you have chosen to become.

Combat, exploration, dialogue and technical interaction are all affected by the active Member configuration.

---

## World

The game takes place in a divided near-future city shaped by extreme technological and social inequality.

The world is structured around four major regions:

- The slums surrounding the Wall
- The Wall itself
- Industrial facilities and Member laboratories
- The Upper City

The visual language combines brutalism, industrial infrastructure, corporate futurism and decaying cyberpunk environments.

---

## Themes

UNSELF explores:

- Identity
- Memory
- Agency
- Artificial personality
- Corporate ownership of the self
- Grief
- Replacement
- Control
- The boundary between player and character

The game does not treat these ideas as background lore.

They are embedded directly into the mechanics.

## SELF

At the beginning, **SELF** appears to be just another missing component.

Something to install.

Something that will make the system complete.

That assumption may be wrong.
